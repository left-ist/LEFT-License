A “Covered Work” means any creation distributed under this License.

* software
* code
* websites
* databases
* texts
* books
* documentation
* graphics
* posters
* visual identities
* fonts
* audio
* video

### 1.3 Preferred Source Form

The form most suitable for modifying the Work.

Examples:

* source code for software
* SVG, Krita, PSD, XCF or equivalent editable files for graphics
* Markdown, ODT, DOCX or equivalent for texts
* project files for audiovisual works
* structured raw data for datasets

To distribute, share, publish, communicate, transmit, display, host, or otherwise make available.

### 1.5 Modify

To adapt, remix, transform, translate, extend, improve, or build upon.

## Article 7 – Emancipatory Alignment Requirement

Rights under this License are granted only where **both**:

1. the specific use of the Work; **and**
2. the User exercising those rights

are reasonably engaged in at least one of the following social aims, and are not materially incompatible with any of them:

* anti-racism
* equality of persons regardless of origin, nationality or belief
* solidarity with migrants and refugees
* labor rights and workplace democracy
* cooperative or democratic ownership
* feminist struggle and gender equality
* LGBTQ+ equality and dignity
* ecological transition and environmental protection
* defense of public services and common goods
* access to knowledge and education
* anti-fascism
* democratic participation and civil liberties
* international solidarity and peace

A claim of alignment based on one objective cannot excuse active hostility to another.
